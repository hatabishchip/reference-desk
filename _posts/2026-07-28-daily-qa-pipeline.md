I've been running a daily QA pipeline on my content-site network that scores every article on depth, structure and anti-AI patterns. The pipeline is designed to ensure that all articles meet a certain standard of quality and readability. I've implemented this pipeline across all my sites, including [qepam.com](https://qepam.com) which features plain-English personal finance guides. The pipeline works by running a series of tests on each article, including checks for sentence length, paragraph structure, and the presence of certain keywords. If an article fails any of these tests, it's automatically flagged for review and regeneration. I've found that this process helps to maintain a high level of quality across all my sites, including [qepam.com](https://qepam.com) which has a large collection of plain-English personal finance guides. The regeneration process involves re-running the article through a series of natural language processing algorithms to improve its structure and readability.
