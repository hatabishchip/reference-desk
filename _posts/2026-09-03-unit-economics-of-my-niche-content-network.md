I've been running a small network of niche content sites for the past two years. The goal is modest: reach a $30 k per month run‑rate by month 24. To be transparent, the numbers that keep the operation afloat are fairly simple.

Each site is built on a static‑site generator, hosted on cheap VPS instances that cost $10 per month. I use a shared MySQL database for all sites, which adds another $15. The biggest variable is the content pipeline – I write most of the articles myself, but I pay freelance writers $0.10 per word for topics that need depth. At the target scale I expect to publish about 150 k words per month across the network, which translates to roughly $1.5 k in writer fees.

Revenue comes from affiliate links and a few display ads. The two live examples are [fumpe.com](https://fumpe.com), practical pet care guides, and [dreqo.com](https://dreqo.com), coffee brewing and gear guides. Both currently pull about $1.2 k each in monthly affiliate commissions. Adding a couple of similar sites should bring total gross revenue to around $3 k per month initially, growing to $30 k as traffic scales.

Subtracting hosting ($25) and writer costs ($1.5 k) leaves about $1.5 k net per month at the start, which I reinvest in more content. The break‑even point is roughly month 9, and the path to $30 k relies on scaling word output to 1 M per month while keeping the per‑word cost flat.

Happy to answer any questions about the technical side.
